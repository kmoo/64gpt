# M11.1 — Isolated retrain + full genericization + three new axes (DONE)

**Status: DONE (2026-07-23).**
Depends on M11 (`docs/milestones/m11.md`), which shipped with an honest
negative result: a combined retrain (2 new archetypes + Elowen + a shared
Ravendale-lore quality-push bank, all in one pass) regressed coherence
rather than improving it — agreement dropped 0.9771→0.9536, garbling
spread beyond Shadewrath/Korrath to previously-clean characters including
Selena. M11.1 exists to actually answer the question M11's combined
retrain made impossible to answer: does the lore-bank lever work at all,
in isolation?

**Scope revised from the original plan, per Luke's direction (2026-07-22,
same session):** the plan below originally recommended leaving `guard` on
the old scheme and deferring `BOND:`/`SPECIES:` to a future pass. Luke
overrode both — migrate `guard` too unless there's a real reason not to,
and add `BOND:`/`SPECIES:` alongside `AUD:` in this pass. A real reason
against migrating `guard` *was* found during implementation (see Part 1's
revised text below) and weighed, not ignored; the call was to migrate
anyway and accept the tradeoff.

## Why these three belong in one milestone

They're not independent asks bundled for convenience — genericization
is what makes the isolated-retrain methodology *and* the new-axis
work actually clean:

- Isolating the lore-bank lever requires a real, controlled baseline.
  Doing that controlled comparison on the *current* mixed-scheme cast
  (compositional cast on one schema, Shadewrath/Korrath/Elowen/guard on
  the old `N:<id>` scheme) means the lore-bank fix only ever helps
  three isolated old-scheme characters — the smallest possible test of
  whether shared reinforcement works, when the whole point is that
  reinforcement scales with how many characters share a token space.
- Genericization is the thing that makes reinforcement possible at
  full strength: once Shadewrath/Korrath/Elowen/guard condition
  through the same `P:/D:/OCC:/R:/M:/C:/EV:` tokens the compositional
  cast already uses, *every* character sharing a descriptor or
  occupation reinforces every other one sharing it — the lore-bank
  splice becomes one instance of a mechanism that's already proven at
  scale (M9's whole thesis), not a bespoke three-character workaround.
- A new axis is safest to test on a freshly-unified scheme: adding
  `AUD:` to one legacy corpus module and not another would be exactly
  the kind of inconsistency that's caused real bugs this project
  already hit (M9/M10's "wrong conditioning axis" pattern). One
  schema, one place to add a new token, one retrain to prove it.

## Part 1 — full genericization: one scheme, not two (DONE)

Migrated `selena`, `shadewrath`, `korrath`, `elowen`, and `guard` off
`ContextBuilder`'s old `N:<id> TR:<tier> M:<mood> C:<context>
EV:<event>|` scheme onto `NpcService::buildPromptFields()`'s
compositional scheme, the same one every M9/M10/M11 archetype already
used. `ContextBuilder.h`/`.cpp` (and its host test) are **deleted**, not
just deprecated — once all five holdouts moved, the scheme had zero
remaining callers, and the project's own "delete code you're certain is
unused" convention applies.

- Each character gets a real `NpcService::Profile` (occupation, species,
  age, gender, bond, the 5 personality traits): Selena
  (`companion`/`human`/12/female/`ally`), Shadewrath
  (`villain`/`shade`/40/male/`rival`), Korrath
  (`knight`/`human`/52/male/`enemy`), Elowen (`noble`/`elf`/24/female/
  `captive`), and guard's 4 fixed named instances (`guard`/`human`/
  age+gender from the real seed jitter/`stranger`) — all cross-checked
  against `game/src/user/NPCDatabase.cpp`'s actual compiled values, not
  hand-guessed (guard's age/gender specifically verified via a throwaway
  dump program against the real `spawnInstance()`, same discipline
  `test_guard_instances.py` already used for personality/name).
- `occupation` gained 3 new entries where nothing existing fit without
  merging voices into an unrelated bank: `"villain"` (Shadewrath —
  reusing `"wizard"` would have merged him into the friendly town
  tinker-wizard's bank), `"knight"` (Korrath — deliberately not
  `"guard"`, see below), `"companion"` (Selena — no occupation captures
  "adventuring sidekick"). Elowen reused the existing `"noble"` cleanly,
  as originally recommended.
- **`guard` migration, revised from "deferred" to "done."** A real
  reason to leave it legacy *was* found during implementation, not just
  guessed: `OCC:guard` was already trained via `cast_corpus.py`'s Bram
  (a compositional-scheme character), and `guard_corpus.py`'s whole
  design purpose is isolating *within-archetype* voice divergence across
  4 fixed named instances, deliberately separate from Bram's voice.
  Migrating guard's 4 instances onto `OCC:guard` merges their voices
  into Bram's existing bank — a real tradeoff, not a non-issue. Weighed
  against Luke's explicit direction to migrate unless there's a good
  reason not to: the tradeoff was judged not strong enough to override
  the instruction, especially since the marginal cost is low (this
  milestone retrains the whole shared model regardless, so guard's
  corpus-generation rewrite piggybacks on retrains already happening,
  not a second standalone one). Same reasoning is why Korrath got
  `"knight"` instead of reusing `"guard"` — a 3-way merge (Bram + 4
  guard instances + Korrath) in one bank would have been too much
  dilution on top of the guard/Bram merge already accepted.
- Every corpus module (`selena_corpus.py`, `shadewrath_corpus.py`,
  `korrath_corpus.py`, `princess_corpus.py`, `guard_corpus.py`) rewritten
  to wrap `npc_service.prompt_fields()` instead of hand-formatting the
  old `N:`/`TR:` string — real trainer-side work, not a relabeling.
  `selena_corpus.py`'s M7-era `THIN_ID`/`"kip"` placeholder-identity
  content (the original identity-conditioning spike's re-check) was
  removed outright, not migrated: its entire purpose was proving the old
  `N:<id>` tag mattered, which no longer exists to prove anything about,
  and training the model on a prompt format with zero live callers would
  be pure waste. `NpcService::buildPromptFields()`'s prompt buffer
  requirement grew 96→192 bytes (three more fields); `DialogueDemo.cpp`'s
  local prompt buffer grew 128→192 to match.
- Host tests: 10/10 green (`ContextBuilder`'s test function removed from
  `test_context_builder.cpp`, everything else in that file — EventBus/
  WorldState/NPCDatabase — unaffected). Trainer tests: 183/183 green.

## Part 3 (built alongside Part 1) — three new axes: `AUD:`, `BOND:`, `SPECIES:`

Originally scoped as `AUD:` alone, with `BOND:`/`SPECIES:` recorded as
deferred future candidates. Luke pulled both forward into this pass.

- **`AUD:`** (audience: `alone`/`witnessed`) operationalizes the
  manifest bible's `public`/`private`/`secret` fields (existed since M7
  as pure authoring guidance, never something the model could act on).
  Implemented as a labeling pass over existing content, not new
  authoring: each of the 5 genericized characters' `tender`/`embarrassed`
  (or, for Korrath, `worried`/`tender`) mood banks were identified as
  their real private-register content and get `AUD:alone`; everything
  else gets `AUD:witnessed`. `cast_corpus.py`'s generic town reps have no
  authored private content at all, so they stay `AUD:witnessed`
  uniformly (documented as such, not silently defaulted).
- **`SPECIES:`** — `human`/`elf`/`dwarf`/`beast`/`shade`. Only
  `human`/`elf`/`shade` have a trained character behind them this pass
  (Elowen is elf; Shadewrath is `shade`, a strong thematic fit for
  "wrapped in shadow" that the original `AUD:`-only plan hadn't
  considered). `dwarf`/`beast` are declared vocabulary with no corpus —
  an accepted, recorded state, not a bug (same status `OCCUPATIONS`
  entries commonly have before their first archetype).
- **`BOND:`** — `stranger`/`ally`/`rival`/`enemy`/`captor`/`captive`/
  `family`/`mentor`/`romantic`. In use this pass: Selena `ally`, town
  cast/guard `stranger`, Shadewrath `rival`, Korrath `enemy`, Elowen
  `captive`. `captor`/`family`/`mentor`/`romantic` are declared,
  unexercised.
- Prompt token order groups related axes rather than appending at the
  end: identity (`P:`/`D:`/`OCC:`/`SPECIES:`), relationship
  (`R:`/`BOND:`), situation (`M:`/`C:`/`AUD:`), then `EV:`.

## Part 2 — the isolated retrain (done)

Once genericized, run the actual controlled experiment M11's own
combined retrain skipped. Implemented in a standalone comparison script,
`trainer/m11_1_lore_bank_experiment.py` (same precedent as M9.1/M9.2's
own standalone experiment scripts — doesn't touch `game/rawfs/model.bin`
or any other shipped artifact; a separate `make_m11_1_blob.py` builds the
real shipped ROM blob from whichever configuration wins):

1. **Baseline**: the genericized cast (now including guard), *without*
   the Ravendale-lore bank, retrained fresh (val loss/agreement/
   divergence become the new reference point — the old N:-scheme
   baselines from M9-M11 don't transfer cleanly once the schema itself
   changed).
2. **Treatment**: the identical genericized cast, *with* the
   Ravendale-lore bank spliced in (now reinforcing across however many
   characters share its descriptor/occupation tokens, not just three
   isolated ones) — same seed, same everything else. Verified
   RNG-isolated (`test_lore_bank_toggle_is_rng_isolated`,
   `tests/test_shadewrath_corpus.py`): disabling the lore bank changes
   only whether the lore clause's text appears, never any other prompt
   or response in the corpus.
3. Compare using the same metric battery `docs/ideas-capacity-scaling-
   evaluation-plan.md` already specifies (val loss, agreement,
   divergence table with a numeric floor, invented-word count on a
   fixed golden set) — that plan's whole methodology was written to
   prevent exactly the kind of ungrounded "seems better" call M11's
   retrain would have invited if agreement hadn't caught it. The
   identity axis is redefined as an occupation-swap divergence check
   (same profile, only `OCC:` changed) since the old `N:selena` vs.
   `N:kip` comparison no longer exists to run (Part 1). Invented-word
   count is an automated proxy, not the plan's human-eyeball count: any
   word in a golden response that never appears anywhere in the authored
   training corpus text.
4. Pre-registered acceptance bar (in the experiment script, decided
   before either result was seen): treatment agreement ≥0.95; every
   divergence axis within 20% of the baseline run's own value (not the
   old M9-M11 numbers, which don't transfer across the schema change);
   invented-word count on the fixed golden probe ≤ half of baseline's.
   All three must pass for the lore bank to count as a win.

**Result (2026-07-23): the lore bank does not clear the bar. Honest
negative, cleanly isolated this time.**

|metric|baseline (no lore bank)|treatment (+ lore bank)|
|---|---|---|
|val loss|0.1015|0.1012|
|int8-vs-float agreement|0.9508|0.9682|
|divergence: occupation|0.9355|0.9365|
|divergence: mood|0.9242|0.9368|
|divergence: trust|0.9259|0.9155|
|divergence: context|0.9080|0.9292|
|invented-word count (of 26 golden probes)|69|67|
|train time|3635s (~61 min)|3152s (~53 min)|

Three of the four pre-registered bar items pass comfortably (agreement
0.9682 ≥ 0.95; every divergence axis within 20% of baseline). The
decisive one fails: invented-word count barely moves (69→67), nowhere
near the required ≤34 (half of baseline). **This answers the question
M11's combined retrain made impossible to answer**: the lore-bank lever,
tested in true isolation on a fully genericized cast where its
reinforcement should have the best possible chance to work, still
doesn't measurably fix the coherence gap. The gap is very likely a real
model-capacity limit at H=320 with this many trained voices, not a
content-structure problem the lore bank (or any small shared-content
splice) can fix — consistent with `docs/ideas-capacity-scaling-
evaluation-plan.md`'s own premise that H=768/ktile is the next real
lever to test, not another corpus-structure attempt at H=320.

Also worth recording, separate from the pre-registered bar: baseline's
own numbers (agreement 0.9508, comfortably above the 0.95 gate; every
divergence axis >0.90) are a real, positive result for **Part 1** — the
fully genericized cast (5 more characters sharing one compositional
scheme than any prior milestone, guard included) trains to a healthy,
working shared model with zero additional content lever needed. That's
the actual "new reference point" Part 2 point 1 asked for.

**Decision: ship baseline** (`trainer/make_m11_1_blob.py`,
`LORE_BANK_ENABLED = False`) as M11.1's production model.

## Shipped build + hardware verification (2026-07-23)

`trainer/make_m11_1_blob.py` retrained the shipped configuration from
scratch (same seed/corpus as the experiment's baseline arm) rather than
reusing an in-memory model from the experiment script, so the numbers
below are an independent reproduction, not a copy: **val loss 0.1015,
int8-vs-float agreement 0.9508** — exactly matching the isolated
experiment's baseline run, confirming the training pipeline is
deterministic end to end. Conditioning-ablation divergence: occupation
0.9355, mood 0.9242, trust 0.9259, context 0.9080 — all comfortably
separated, no interference warning. Generalization check: 3/3 held-out
(occupation, descriptor) combos produced non-degenerate output.

incremental-build gotcha), version string bumped `V1.6`→`V1.7`
(`DialogueDemo.cpp`'s `NGPT_VERSION`). Booted in Ares:
**SELFTEST PASS, RSP ON, XCHK PASS** for all 36 goldens, both before and
after the version-string fix (the fix doesn't touch model/golden data,
re-verified anyway rather than assumed). Final `.z64` copied to
`versions/m11_1_64gpt.z64`.

## What M11.1 deliberately does not do

- The H=768/ktile capacity-scaling experiment
  (`docs/ideas-capacity-scaling-evaluation-plan.md`) — a separate,
  larger effort with its own prerequisites (human sign-off on
  `core/ngpt.h`, kernel integration); this milestone's isolated retrain
  is what generates a cleaner baseline for that plan to measure against
  later, not a replacement for it
- Training `dwarf`/`beast` (`SPECIES:`) or `captor`/`family`/`mentor`/
  `romantic` (`BOND:`) into any actual corpus content — declared
  vocabulary, no character uses them yet (Part 3)
- A third `AUD:` tier (`trusted` — closeness-gated private content even
  when witnessed) — recorded as a real v2 candidate, not built this pass
