# M12.2 — Richer voices: an honest negative + infrastructure wins

**Status: LANDED (2026-07-25) as a code/docs commit — NOT a ROM/model
release.** What shipped: the agreement-gate fix, the test hardening, and
this finding. What did NOT ship: the richer character voices — they
regress coherence at H=320 and are **deferred to M12.3** (per-step
attribute embeddings, `docs/ideas-m12.3-conditioning-strategies.md`). The
voice-corpus edits themselves are preserved on branch `m12.3-voices` as
M12.3's starting point. Depends on M12.1 (`docs/milestones/m12.1.md`).

## What we tried

A pass over the six corpus generators to sharpen each cast voice —
**guards funnier, Shadewrath colder/more menacing, Selena sassier, Elowen
more noble/elegant** — kids-appropriate, no scheme/blob/engine change.
Pure data: edit the phrase banks, retrain, re-verify. (Most of the corpus
*growth* was a pending bank expansion already in the tree; the M12.2 tone
edits on top are length-neutral in-place rewrites.)

## The honest negative

The retrain reached the real coherence gate and **failed it**: **1.65–1.85
invented words/line (raw model) vs the ≤1.0 gate M12.1 cleared** (still
better than M12's 2.6, but a clear regression). It is **deterministic** —
the float phase reproduced run-to-run exactly — so it is a real property
of the richer corpus, not a bad training draw.

The trie guard makes this subtle. Measured on the same probe prompts:

| output | invented words/line |
|---|---|
| raw model (no trie) | 1.65 |
| **trie-guarded (what would ship)** | **0.00** |

Zero invented words shipped — but reading the trie-guarded text shows
**word-salad**: real words spliced into grammatical mush after the first
clause (e.g. *"I'LL GIVE YOU MY ANSWER, LET IT HEAL IN ABOUT A TROLL, I
CAN TELL, FINDERS KNOW THIS GOES…"*). The trie guarantees *real words*, not
*coherent sentences*. So the ≤1.0 raw gate is doing its job — it flags
that the model would be garbage without the trie, i.e. it's leaning on the
guard rather than earning coherence. We did **not** relax it (that would
ship visibly worse dialogue; contrast the agreement gate below, which was
genuinely mis-set).

**Root cause:** the corpus got richer/more varied than an H=320 model can
coherently fit under the current *prefix* conditioning. This is the same
capacity wall M10/M11.1/M12 hit, now from the data side — and it is
exactly what M12.3's per-step attribute embeddings target (help the model
*use* its capacity instead of memorizing a longer prefix it then forgets).
Rather than trim the corpus to fit — a treadmill — we take this as the
empirical case for the architectural fix.

## What DID land (unambiguous, corpus-independent)

- **Agreement-gate fix** (`make_m12_1_blob.py`). The int-vs-float top-1
  agreement gate is **corpus-coupled** (its probe is the held-out Selena
  lines; completion-only agreement falls as lines grow): the *shipped
  M12.1 model* scores 0.9836 on M12.1's corpus but 0.8873 on the grown
  corpus, identical charset. A 0.95 floor thus spuriously FATALs known-good
  models. Demoted to a catastrophic-collapse backstop (`MIN_AGREEMENT =
  0.70`), matching its own comments' intent; real quality stays gated on
  the coherence probe + greedy parity.
- **Test hardening (all mutation-verified — proven to fail on a real bug):**
  - `tests/test_trie_sampler.cpp` Fixture D — the trie guard's word-boundary
    rule (boundary bytes illegal mid-word, legal only at end-of-word,
    reset-to-root), previously untested.
  - `tests/test_blob_parser.cpp` (5 tests) — the v2 (trie-carrying) blob
    format's parse path, which had no positive test.
  - `trainer/tests/test_corpus_charset.py` (4 tests) — corpus charset in
    the allowed set, all-uppercase, vocab ≤ `NGPT_GRU_MAX_VOCAB`, and the
    trie contains exactly every corpus word.
- **M12.3 plan** (`docs/ideas-m12.3-conditioning-strategies.md`).

## For M12.3

The richer-voice corpus on `m12.3-voices` is the target M12.3 must make
coherent. Success criterion is the same ≤1.0 raw-model coherence probe
this milestone failed — reached this time via per-step attribute
embeddings rather than a bigger prefix. Spike host-only in `ref_impl.py`
first (no N64 changes) and measure against this exact probe before any
blob/kernel work.
